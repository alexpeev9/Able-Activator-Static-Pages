Demo Day / 15 Apr 2027 / 7PM – 11PM Wed

Grand Final Pitch Event

Topics covered
  • Grand Final Pitch Event
  • Hand-picked Jury from the Start-up Ecosystem
  • Representatives of the ABLE Community, Sponsors & Partners
  • Announcement of the Best-performing Team

Demo Day is the culmination of ABLE Activator. Teams deliver their final pitches in front of a specially selected jury of industry leaders and representatives of the Bulgarian start-up ecosystem with a proven track record.

Each team has 5 minutes to showcase their product or service, followed by a 5-minute Q&A with the jury. Each project's potential, progress and impact is measured, and the best-performing team of the edition is announced.

The day is a celebration of everything the teams accomplish: self-exploration, team formation and the hard work behind first milestones such as an MVP, first customers or revenue — the grit and determination to build something new with the right product-market fit.

The evening
Wednesday 15 Apr
7:00 PM – 11:00 PM

Each team pitches for five minutes to a hand-picked jury of founders and investors, in front of the ABLE community, sponsors and partners. The evening closes with the announcement of the best-performing team of the edition.
